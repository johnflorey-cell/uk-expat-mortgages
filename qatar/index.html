Why expats in Qatar use a specialist broker

Many UK high street lenders decline applicants living overseas or offer them only limited products. We search the wider market, including specialist and private banks, to find lenders that accept residents of Qatar.

Qatari riyal income accepted

The Qatari riyal is pegged to the US dollar, and a number of UK lenders accept QAR income for UK mortgages.

Sector experience

Many expats in Qatar work in energy, aviation, construction, healthcare and education. We understand the contracts and allowances typical of these roles.

Tax-free salaries understood

Employment income in Qatar is generally not subject to personal income tax. We present tax-free packages clearly so lenders assess them properly.

What we can help with

UK mortgage questions from expats in Qatar

Answers to the questions we are asked most often.

Can I get a UK mortgage if I live in Qatar?

Yes. Several UK lenders accept Qatar residents, including British expats and foreign nationals. We check which lenders fit your income, employment and deposit before you apply.

Will lenders accept my income in Qatari riyals?

Many specialist lenders accept QAR income, sometimes applying a small adjustment for exchange rate movements.

Can I buy a UK investment property from Qatar?

Yes. Expat buy-to-let and limited company purchases are common, and we can guide you through both routes.

How much deposit will I need?

For expats and foreign nationals, lenders usually require a deposit of between 25% and 30% of the purchase price, depending on the lender, your residency, income and the type of property.
