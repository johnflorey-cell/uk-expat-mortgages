Why expats in China use a specialist broker

Many UK high street lenders decline applicants living overseas or offer them only limited products. We search the wider market, including specialist and private banks, to find lenders that accept residents of China.

Renminbi income accepted by selected lenders

A smaller number of UK lenders accept income paid in renminbi, so knowing which lenders will consider China residents is the key first step.

Plan your deposit transfer early

China's foreign exchange rules limit how much individuals can convert and send abroad each year, so we recommend planning your deposit transfer early and taking local advice.

UK mortgage questions from expats in China

Answers to the questions we are asked most often.

Could I get a UK mortgage if I live in China?

Yes, although fewer UK lenders accept China residents than some other countries. A specialist broker takes you straight to lenders that will consider your residency and income.

Will UK lenders accept my income in renminbi?

Some specialist lenders accept CNY income, usually with an allowance for exchange rate movements. Income paid in US dollars, Hong Kong dollars or sterling can widen your options.

Can I buy a UK property for my child studying in the UK?

Often, yes. There are several ways to structure a purchase for a family member at university in the UK, and we can explain which lenders and structures fit your situation.

How much deposit will I need?

For expats and foreign nationals, lenders usually require a deposit of between 25% and 30% of the purchase price, depending on the lender, your residency, income and the type of property.
