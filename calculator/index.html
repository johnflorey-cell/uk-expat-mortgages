How expat lenders look at your mortgage

The calculator gives a guide. Lenders then apply their own rules, which is why the right lender matters so much for expats.

Loan to value

Expat lenders cap borrowing at 75% of the property value, so you will need a deposit, or equity if remortgaging, of at least 25%. The best rates are often found at 60% to 65%.

Rental cover

For buy-to-let, lenders test that the rent covers the interest at a stress rate. Individual buyers usually need 145% cover and limited company (SPV) buyers 125%. The mortgage is the lower of this rent-based figure and 75% of the value, so the rent often sets the limit.

Term and fixed rate period

The term is the total length of the mortgage, from 2 years up to 25 years. The fixed rate period, usually 2 or 5 years, is how long your interest rate is set for. When it ends, the mortgage moves to the lender's variable rate unless you choose another fixed rate, and we can help you arrange that.

Interest only or repayment

Interest only is the most popular choice for buy-to-let and investment property, keeping monthly costs down and helping the rent cover the mortgage. The loan is repaid at the end of the term, so you need a repayment plan. Repayment mortgages clear the loan over the term.

Foreign currency income

Income in AED, SAR, USD, HKD and other currencies is widely accepted, though some lenders reduce it by 10% to 25% to allow for exchange rate movements.
