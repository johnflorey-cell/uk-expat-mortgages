Expat mortgage questions

Answers to the questions we are asked most often.

Can I get a UK mortgage if I live abroad?

Yes. Many UK lenders will lend to British expats and foreign nationals living overseas, but criteria vary widely. A specialist broker can match you to lenders that accept your country of residence, income currency and employment type.

Can I remortgage my UK property while living overseas?

Yes. Expats can remortgage to a better rate, release equity or move from a residential mortgage to buy-to-let. Your existing lender may not offer its best products to overseas residents, so comparing the wider market matters.

How much deposit does an expat need for a UK mortgage?

For expats and foreign nationals, lenders usually require a deposit of between 25% and 30% of the purchase price, depending on the lender, your residency, income and the type of property.

Is income paid in a foreign currency accepted?

Many specialist lenders accept income in currencies such as AED, SAR, BHD, QAR, USD, HKD and SGD, sometimes applying a small reduction to allow for exchange rate movement.

Can I buy through a limited company or SPV?

Yes. Buy-to-let purchases through a UK limited company (SPV) are common for expat investors, and some lenders also consider offshore holding structures.
